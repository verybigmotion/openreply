# Own Meta app in Development mode instead of Zernio

OpenReply supports two providers: Zernio (paid, no Meta review, 25-post picker limit, paid analytics) and a self-owned Meta app (free, but App Review plus business verification before strangers can connect). With about ten known influencers we run our own Meta app under the appscale Meta Business portfolio and keep it in Development mode: every influencer is added as an Instagram Tester by hand, which removes the need for App Review. Providers cannot be migrated later, so growing past what manual Tester invites can handle means either passing App Review or reconnecting every account through Zernio.
