# Facebook Pages connect only through the own Meta app

Facebook Pages are Social accounts with the same Campaign behaviour as Instagram, apart from the follow gate. They connect through the appscale Meta app from ADR 0001: the personal Facebook profile that administers the Page is added as a Tester, and Facebook Login for Business grants the Page permissions. Zernio stays Instagram-only, so a Page cannot be connected through Zernio, and moving Pages to Zernio later would mean building and testing that provider path first.
